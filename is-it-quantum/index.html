Case file XEB-5

Is it quantum?

Two machines say they ran your circuit. One of them is faking it.

Starting up.

How this works

A quantum computer running a random circuit gives a stream of answers that looks like noise. So does a faker flipping coins. This page runs four random circuits on the machine you picked, lines its answers up against three fakers, and shows the test that tells them apart.

If nothing appears here, press Run and choose the built-in simulator.
