Warming the press.

How this works

The bank prints a note whose serial number is eight qubits. Each qubit sits either straight or diagonal, and only the bank knows which. Your job is to turn one note into two that both get past the teller.

Every note, every forgery and every check on this page is a real circuit run on the machine you picked. If nothing appears here, press Run and choose the built-in simulator.
